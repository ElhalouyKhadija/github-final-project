# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards
Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing opinions
- Accepting constructive criticism

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:
- Harassment or discrimination
- Personal attacks
- Public or private harassment

## Enforcement Responsibilities
Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior.

## Scope
This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces and public spaces when an individual is representing the project.

## Enforcement
Instances of abusive or unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project team.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly.

## Enforcement Guidelines
Maintainers will follow fair and consistent actions, such as:
- Warning the offender
- Temporary ban
- Permanent ban
